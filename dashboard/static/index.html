About & Methodology
How the prediction is made, what data feeds it, and who built it.

The Day to Day Stock Price Predictor estimates whether Microsoft's (MSFT) closing price will move up or down on the next trading day. It combines three machine-learning models — a Random Forest, an XGBoost classifier, and an LSTM neural network — into a stacking ensemble that learns when each underlying model is most trustworthy. Inputs include MSFT daily prices and three macro signals (Gold, Crude Oil, and the VIX volatility index). The dashboard is built for retail investors who want a simple directional signal without wading through code or formulas.
